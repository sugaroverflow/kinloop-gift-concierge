# Kinloop Product Brief

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

Kinloop is an agentic gift approval cockpit. It imports relationship signals from an agent-owned inbox, uses Codex to transform messy context into structured gift options, and keeps the final approval human.

## Users

- A busy gift giver who wants to remember important dates and make thoughtful choices on time.
- A hackathon evaluation panel looking for a working, creative, technically credible Codex application.

## Primary Story

Sarah's birthday is close. A hint arrives at `kinloop-agent@agentmail.to`. Kinloop imports the hint, extracts the relationship signal, runs Codex Signal Studio, presents three gift options, and records one human approval.

## Product Principles

- One complete flow beats many partial features.
- Real signal intake matters more than broad shopping.
- Codex is a bounded runtime transformation layer, not a magic text box.
- Approval is explicit, auditable, and separate from purchase/payment/fulfillment.
- The interface should feel polished, calm, and technically impressive.

## Anti-References

- Marketplace clone.
- Shopping basket.
- Checkout flow.
- Admin dashboard.
- Internal build labels in the product surface.
- Stock-photo-heavy ecommerce page.

## Tone

Human, precise, quietly futuristic. The product should feel like a trusted operations cockpit for thoughtful gifting.
