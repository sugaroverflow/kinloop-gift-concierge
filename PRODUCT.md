# Kinloop Product Brief

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

Kinloop is a gift concierge for people who care about birthdays but do not want to manage the logistics by hand. It imports relationship sources, identifies the people and dates that matter, turns clues into gift ideas, and keeps approval with the user.

## Users

- A busy gift giver who wants thoughtful ideas before a birthday deadline.
- A technical evaluator looking for a credible AI-enabled product with real architecture under the surface.

## Primary Story

A user signs in, imports connected relationship sources, reviews the people Kinloop found, edits any missing essentials, reveals gift ideas for the highest-priority birthday, approves one, and opts into a reminder call before the decision deadline.

## Product Principles

- Consumer product first. Architecture belongs in code and docs, not in the shopper-facing UI.
- Source-driven suggestions beat generic shopping.
- Gift approval is explicit and separate from purchasing, payment, and fulfillment.
- The interface should feel sleek, composed, and useful in under one minute.
- Technical ambition should appear through real behavior: structured source import, product-feed-backed ideas, persistence, tests, and bounded AI generation.

## Anti-References

- Marketplace clone.
- Shopping basket.
- Checkout flow.
- Admin dashboard.
- Demo console.
- Internal build labels in the product surface.
- Stock-photo-heavy ecommerce page.

## Tone

Precise, warm, quietly futuristic. Kinloop should feel like a trusted personal system, not a backend control panel.
